02 / Ready to work

A useful toolkit from the first boot.

Grizzly brings together everyday development, communication, and desktop essentials. Keep what works, change what doesn’t.

  • Develop: VSCodium, Git, Lazygit
  • Build: Docker, Compose, Sandbox (sbx)
  • Use the terminal: Foot
  • Browse: Firefox
  • Keep credentials: 1Password
  • Connect: OpenSSH, disabled by default
  • Manage files: Dolphin, Double Commander
  • Meet and create: Easy Effects, Paint.software
  • Everyday work: LibreOffice, 7-Zip
  • Customize: system settings, updates, themes, keybindings

By design: Grizzly does not bundle an AI harness or a Docker management frontend. For a safe agentic development experience we strongly recommend something like the Docker Sandbox so your agent can run wild with less risk of something harmful being done on your system without you knowing about it. For container management, we prefer tools like Portainer and Dozzle.
