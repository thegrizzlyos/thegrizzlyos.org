GRIZZLY

Your AI development OS 🐾

01 / THE IDEA

Built for focus

Grizzly is an opinionated OS aiming to maximize developer productivity by removing nearly all normal desktop GUI and keeping only what's truly important: the focus on your work.

The tiling concept makes your apps dock into place, and you can have up to 10 workspaces pre-configured for how you work.

This is fundamentally a much better way to work for developers than the broken desktop metaphor.

Grizzly is built on Debian, inheriting decades of rock-solid Linux, with performance optimized out of the box.

Shipping with an extensible CLI to maintain your system and support your own workflows.

Built-in tools for tile sharing.

Theme-compatible with Omarchy and using many of the same keybindings.

Installs extremely fast.
